What is Effective Communication? And How can you achieve that?

A practical breakdown of what it means to communicate effectively — and the habits you can build to master it.

What is effective communication

Effective communication is a skill that helps you to cooperate more clearly with others. It is a way to convey your message more easily and effectively. It is about making the person understand rather than only passing information.

Why effective communication is important for us

When we talk, sometimes we forget that the person in front of us is also human and he/she has feelings too. We just keep talking for our own satisfaction, but the person feels bored and tired. For that, we should learn to make the person comfortable while talking so that they will be interested in our talk and listen carefully.

How you Can achieve this Skill?

Four core habits that will transform the way people receive your message.

01

Body language

Body language refers to the way we move our body parts while talking. Sometimes we ignore this and move our body however we want. But it gives a bad impression to the listener, and the person will be less interested in our talk. For that, we should move our body as little as possible and try to be formal. And while we talk, we can use our hands to make it feel more professional and formal. Also, if we are having any meetings, we should avoid negative body language. Now, what do I mean by that? Sometimes while the person is talking, suddenly we get an itching problem and try to rub rapidly; we should strongly avoid that and try to control it as much as we can. And sometimes we try to set our hair. We don't need to look dashing to the person; our goal is to convey and receive information, not to look cool and flirty.

02

Listen carefully while the person talks

Sometimes we interrupt while someone is talking. We should strongly avoid that everywhere, not only in official life but also in daily life. We should be calm and listen to every word whatever the person is saying, no matter if we agree or disagree with that. Once the person is done with his/her talk, we can start talking.

03

Never argue aggressively

Sometimes there is a point where our thoughts do not match. In that situation, we should stay calm and talk politely and try to make the person understand why he is wrong. And if there is no strong reason to argue, we should compromise and agree with the person.

04

Eye movement

Eye movement is also important for effective communication. While we talk or the person talks, we should look straight into the person's eyes. Sometimes while someone is talking, we are busy on our phone or looking at the ceiling fan and like, "wow, the fan is rotating, damn." We should strongly avoid these types of behavior, especially while talking to our elders or talking for work-related stuff.
